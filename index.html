Own the season.

Voltball is a fantasy league built on your Zappies Reborn collection. A Hero NFT coaches your team, seven of your held Zappies fill the roster, and every week's formation decides how the match plays out.

Requires a Hero (or collab) NFT and 7+ roster-eligible Zappies in your wallet.
01

Register your coach

Connect your wallet and register a team under whichever Hero NFT you hold — that Hero coaches your team for the season.

02

Set your formation

Pick OFFENSE, BALANCED, or DEFENSE each week and slot 7 held Zappies into Surge Back, Conductor, and Guard lanes.

03

Watch it play out

Matches resolve quarter by quarter in real time. Check Standings any time to see how your team stacks up.

Already registered? Connecting your wallet takes you straight to your lineup.
